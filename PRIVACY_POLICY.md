# Privacy Policy for FBI Content Blocker

Last updated: August 25, 2026

FBI Content Blocker is designed to work locally in Firefox. This policy explains how the add-on handles information.

## Information used locally

The add-on checks the address of the top-level page you visit against the enabled domain lists so it can decide whether to show its local block page. This check happens inside your browser.

Your selected filter categories are stored locally in Firefox using the browser's local extension storage. They are used only to remember your preferences.

The domain lists are packaged with the add-on and loaded locally. Neither the lists nor your filter selections are shared with the developer or with third parties.

## No runtime data sharing

The add-on does not send, sell, upload, transmit, or share your browsing activity, visited addresses, filter choices, domain lists, personal information, analytics, diagnostics, or usage data with the developer or any third party.

The project maintainer may manually download public source lists while preparing a new add-on release. That maintenance process is separate from the installed add-on and does not send data from your browser.

## Third-party websites

When a site is not blocked, your browser connects to that site under its own privacy practices. This add-on does not control those websites.

## Changes to this policy

If the add-on's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or with the release that introduces the change.
